About TWG

Social Values

Building better places
and leaving a positive mark on the communities we work in.

Our approach

Delivering social value through our projects

Our projects create new homes and buildings, and we know that while we are on site our work can affect the people who live and work nearby. Across Roofworx, Woodworx and Fire-worx we aim to keep that impact as low as possible through careful planning and consideration, and to leave a positive legacy in every area we work in.

What we commit to

Our Social Value Pledge

Across The Worx Group we are committed to:

  • Investing in training and apprenticeship programmes, giving the next generation hands-on experience, industry knowledge and mentorship
  • Using local suppliers and local labour wherever we can, to support communities and reduce our carbon footprint
  • Sourcing all timber and sheet materials from PEFC and/or FSC certified suppliers
  • Organising and carrying out our works to reduce noise and dust as far as reasonably possible
  • Keeping our operatives and everyone affected by our work safe, with CSCS-carded operatives and SMSTS / SSSTS-qualified site managers and supervisors

People & skills

Training & Apprenticeships

We believe in investing in the future of our industry. Our training and apprenticeship programmes give aspiring roofers, carpenters and fire door specialists hands-on experience, industry knowledge and mentorship from our experienced team - fostering growth in the next generation and ensuring a skilled workforce for years to come.

Environment

Delivering on our promises

We ensure all our staff and carpenters work in an environmentally and socially responsible way. All timber and sheet materials come from PEFC and/or FSC certified suppliers, and we seek to use local suppliers and local labour to support communities and reduce our carbon footprint.

Supporting our partners

Make-A-Wish UK · 2027 TCS London Marathon

We are proud to support Tom Brennan, Managing Surveyor at our client HG Construction, who is running the London Marathon in April 2027 for the Make-A-Wish Foundation UK - a charity that supported a close friend of his through tough times. Tom is starting his training from barely being able to run 3K.

Make-A-Wish UK grants carefully chosen wishes to children living with critical illness, creating moments of excitement, strength and happiness for them and their families. Tom's fundraising target is £3,000.
