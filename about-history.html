About TWG

History

Three companies, one full envelope contractor

The Worx Group brings together three specialist companies (Roofworx, Woodworx and Fire-worx) to give clients one point of contact across the whole building envelope. Based in Bristol and delivering nationwide, we help main contractors and developers reduce interfaces, simplify responsibility on site and hand over a high-quality finished building on programme. Our teams bring specialist expertise, reliable delivery and a shared commitment to getting it right first time.

Roofworx is a roofing, cladding and waterproofing specialist with over 20 years’ experience, delivering projects nationwide from Glasgow to Plymouth. We work with national contractors and developers on large, technically demanding packages, supporting them from design through to handover with the right system for every roof. Our clients rely on us to deliver on time, to a high standard and with roofs that are built to last and guaranteed.

Client: “The three roofers from Roofworx have been a great help and worked long days to push on the work.”

Woodworx delivers first- and second-fix carpentry and joinery for commercial and residential clients, with a team of over 55 skilled carpenters. From structural carpentry and staircases to internal fit-out and bespoke joinery, we combine craftsmanship with dependable programme delivery and attention to detail. All timber and sheet materials come from PEFC and/or FSC certified suppliers, and working alongside Roofworx lets clients bring envelope and carpentry packages together under one group.

Client: “Knowledgeable, effective and hardworking – couldn’t ask for more.”

Fire-worx is our fire door specialist, providing supply, installation, inspection, maintenance and testing for commercial and residential buildings. Our skilled team works to current regulations and industry best practice, helping clients achieve compliant, reliable passive fire protection that keeps people and buildings safe. We focus on getting every door right first time and building long-term relationships based on trust.

Director of Nursing & Care Services: “Very impressed with the overall look of the doors supplied by Fire-worx.”
