Quality & safety

Accreditations

Our accreditations are independent checks on how we manage health and safety and quality, and how we treat our clients.

CHAS

Contractors Health and Safety Assessment Scheme. An SSIP-member scheme that assesses contractors against health and safety legislation, giving clients confidence in our safety management.

SSIP

Safety Schemes in Procurement. The umbrella body that lets member health and safety pre-qualification schemes recognise each other, cutting duplicated assessments for clients and contractors.

SMAS Worksafe

An SSIP-registered health and safety pre-qualification scheme that independently assesses contractors' health and safety policies and procedures.

Constructionline Gold

Constructionline is the UK register of pre-qualified construction suppliers. Gold membership adds enhanced checks aligned to the PAS 91 pre-qualification standard.

TrustMark

The government-endorsed quality scheme for trades working in and around the home. Registered businesses are vetted for technical competence, customer service and trading practices.
